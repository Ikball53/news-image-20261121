# The World Today — Saturday 21 November 2026

A newspaper front page that sums up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image: nine illustrated story cards (missiles down in Poland, the Kryvyi Rih strike, the UK–EU reset deal, intercepted Houthi missiles over Riyadh, Rohingya resettlement after the ICJ ruling, Indonesia's $30B fire losses, India's NSE becoming the world's 5th-largest exchange, a pancreatic-cancer pill breakthrough, and India's fightback in the Wellington Test), a world-map panel with red dots marking where each story happened, and a ticker with two more of the day's stories.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` — deterministic, no network.
